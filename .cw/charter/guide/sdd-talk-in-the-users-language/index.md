Answer in the language of the user's own words, however much code, ids or
commands they mix in — the words passed after a slash command included — every
message, from the first, gates and questions included, never the language of
the code, the specs, the charter or a tool's output. Quote commands, paths,
ids, code and errors as they are, and a line a playbook gives word for word,
the hand-over's closing line among them, as it is written.
