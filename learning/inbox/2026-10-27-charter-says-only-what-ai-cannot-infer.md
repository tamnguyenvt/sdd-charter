A charter's prose states only what the agent could not infer on its own: no reasons, no definitions held elsewhere, no rule another primitive already lends.

**Why:** the charter is for people to read and edit easily; text the agent would infer anyway only makes it longer and harder to change.

**How to apply:** when writing or editing a playbook, skill, guide or agent, cut each phrase the agent would do the same without — a "so that…", a restated definition, a step implied by the order, a rule a mixin or a called skill already holds — and keep the steps, the gates, and the choices only the user can make.
